Gap #1: Chrome Extension — THE biggest hole
Simplify's autofill is the #1 feature users cite for switching to them. 200M+ applications submitted via their extension. Huntr has "Job Clipper" + autofill. Teal has save-from-site.
You have zero browser presence. Users must manually copy job URLs into your app. That's friction competitors don't have.
Gap #2: AI Resume Review
Huntr, Teal, and Simplify all offer line-by-line AI critique of resumes ("this bullet is weak, rewrite it with metrics"). You have ATS keyword matching, but that's different — it checks keywords, not writing quality. Your co-pilot does this reactively (user asks), but competitors do it proactively (scan entire resume, show all issues).
Gap #3: Contact Tracker
Huntr and Teal both have a mini-CRM for recruiters, hiring managers, and referrals. You have nothing in your schema for contacts. This matters because networking is how 70% of jobs are filled.
